# License FAQ

### Can you summarize what Companion Hermes's license allows?

The license grants free rights to use, modify, and redistribute Companion Hermes source code for personal or nonprofit purposes. The intent is to stay permissive for those uses while limiting commercial abuse of Companion Intelligence products.

Companion Hermes is packaging and deployment tooling for running Hermes Agent (upstream, MIT-licensed by Nous Research) as a Companion Hub Marketplace app.

Companion Hermes uses the [PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0](https://polyformproject.org/licenses/noncommercial/1.0.0). The full text is in [`LICENSE`](../LICENSE).

### What freedoms do you have as a Companion Hermes user?

If you use Companion Hermes for personal or nonprofit purposes, you can view, modify, and redistribute the source under the same license terms, subject to the PolyForm Noncommercial restrictions.

### Can I fork Companion Hermes?

Yes. For noncommercial use, you can fork, modify, and redistribute Companion Hermes under the same license.

### Can I contribute to Companion Hermes?

Yes. Open a pull request on this repository.

### Can I sell Companion Hermes?

No. Selling Companion Hermes, including paid access to a hosted instance, is not permitted. For commercial partnership questions, email partner@companionintelligence.com.

### Does this license apply to the rest of Companion Intelligence?

No. Companion Portal stays a separate commercial product. Most other Companion Intelligence product repositories use the same PolyForm Noncommercial terms in their own repositories — see the org's open-source rollout tracker for the current list.

### Unsure whether your use case is allowed?
